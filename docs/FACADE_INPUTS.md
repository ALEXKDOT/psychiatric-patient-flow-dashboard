# Getting window and façade linework for Flow Atlas

Start with one named building and one visible wall. For that wall, the smallest usable handoff is **one current, scaled exterior elevation** (a drawing of the building viewed straight on), **its location on the mapped footprint**, and **permission to publish the traced lines**. A set of photographs can substitute when it shows the windows clearly and includes enough measured points to correct perspective. Either route can produce a static, self-hosted asset. Visitors need no account, API key or connection to an imagery service.

The request list below covers one representative building at each of the 23 campuses. For a complete rotating model, repeat the handoff for every exposed wall, including upper setbacks and wings. Surrounding buildings need their own evidence if window-level detail is wanted there. A single frontage can ship as partial coverage.

## What to send

| If you have | Deliver | What the atlas can show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An owner's or architect's record drawings | The relevant exterior sheets as a dimensioned vector PDF or DXF/DWG, their issue/revision date, scale or dimensions, and a north-oriented key plan identifying each wall and its endpoints. Request as-built or recent surveyed conditions. | Window groups, recessed bays, horizontal bands and roof edges depicted on the named sheets. Record whether a later renovation changed them. |
| An exterior 3D model | IFC with exterior windows/curtain-wall elements, CityGML with explicit openings, or GLB/glTF/OBJ with separable edges or opening geometry. Supply units, axes, ground reference and placement. Include referenced files for multipart formats. A sample should show actual window geometry at the intended viewing scale. | The modeled faces, openings and levels. A plain shell or a photo texture alone still needs interpretation before it yields individual opening outlines. |
| Photographs you own or can adapt | Original JPEG/TIFFs with capture date, photographer and marked viewing directions. For a flat wall, include a nearly front-on full view and four well-spaced points on that plane with known wall coordinates, plus a separate check dimension or point. For stepped, curved or partly obscured walls use overlapping views with surveyed control, or provide a scaled orthophoto for each face. | Visible detail in the photographed areas. Treat unmeasured depth, hidden window runs and faces outside the photograph as unknown. [Survey guidance, sections 3–4](https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/geospatial-survey-specifications-cultural-heritage/) |
| A survey deliverable | Registered street/terrestrial LiDAR (E57, LAS or LAZ) with enough points on the vertical faces, paired with aligned photographs or completed 3D linework. Supply capture date, spatial reference, units, registration error and a sample from a glass wall. Request rectified wall images or DXF linework if you want the vendor to do the tracing. | Edges that the scan or accompanying imagery actually resolves. Check reflective glazing, upper storeys and blocked sightlines in the sample. |

**Registration.** Put the campus ID and source building ID on each handoff. Identify the wing and face on a site sketch. For local drawing coordinates, provide a labeled ground origin, ground elevation convention, floor/vertical units and a second mapped corner establishing direction and scale. For georeferenced material, provide the horizontal coordinate reference system, units, height datum and whether heights are absolute or above local ground. The registry's longitude/latitude point identifies the building. A single point cannot establish model rotation or photo scale. [Target registry](../src/cartography/campus/targets.json)

**Rights.** Supply a license URL or a written grant from the rights holder identifying the files. It must cover extracting and editing the exterior linework, storing it locally, and distributing the finished geometry in a public downloadable web app for offline use. Identify the required credit, modification notice, share-alike conditions and any term or expiry. If the raw photos or drawings must stay private, a grant covering publication of the derived geometry is sufficient. Ask separately about permission to display raw imagery only if you want it shown. An acquisition account or a subscription alone establishes none of these rights.

**Coverage and accuracy.** List each wall with its source sheet or photo ID, capture date, known dimensions, which portion is visible and any gaps. For a photo-derived wall, compare an independently known dimension or corner against the registered trace and record the discrepancy in metres. Review an overlay from the source viewpoint and from a second angle where available. Publish only supported lines. For an unknown area, the mapped mass can still appear with a coverage note. There is no project-wide claim of survey accuracy.

**Copyable request for an owner or survey provider**

> We are building Flow Atlas, a public web map that can display locally stored 3D hospital exteriors offline. For [building name and ID], can you supply a dated existing-conditions exterior elevation or model showing window openings, bays, setbacks and roof edges on [requested faces]? Please include scale, wall orientation, a site sketch or mapped corner coordinates, and any known coverage gaps. We need permission to trace, simplify and distribute the resulting exterior linework as downloadable files in the public app. Please identify the rights holder, required credit, duration, delivery format and any fee. A single wall and a sample of the license terms would let us evaluate fit.

## Other sources and their limits

**NYC Open Data, free massing for the 22 city targets.** NYC OTI publishes a citywide CityGML ZIP, ESRI Multipatch and MicroStation DGN. It was a one-time aerial capture of buildings present in **2014**. Most structures use a hybrid of levels of detail 1 and 2. About 100 iconic buildings have more roof detail. The dataset identifies wall planes and roof edges but publishes no specification for surveyed window openings. Join by BIN/OTI ID, check present-day footprint and vintage, and retain the source, version and modifications under NYC Open Data terms. NYC Planning's Rhino files are another rendering of the same survey. A concrete coverage gap is South Brooklyn's Ruth Bader Ginsburg Hospital, which opened in 2023. [City dataset and formats](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/3-D-Building-Model/tnru-abg2/about_data) · [CityGML ZIP](https://s-media.nyc.gov/agencies/oti/DA_WISE_GML.zip) · [OTI specifications](https://github.com/CityOfNewYork/nyc-geo-metadata/blob/main/Metadata/Metadata_3DBuildingModel.md) · [NYC Open Data policy](https://cityofnewyork.github.io/opendatatsm/publicpolicies.html) · [Planning's Rhino metadata](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/planning/download/pdf/data-maps/open-data/nyc-3d-model-metadata.pdf) · [South Brooklyn opening](https://www.nychealthandhospitals.org/pressrelease/nyc-health-hospitals-south-brooklyn-health-celebrates-the-opening-of-the-new-ruth-bader-ginsburg-hospital/)

**Cyclomedia, conditional commercial coverage.** The city has used the vendor's street-level imagery and LiDAR. Ask Cyclomedia US at **info-us@cyclomedia.com** for a dated coverage map and a sample for the listed building/wing, with upper-floor and obscured-area coverage marked. Request either a licensed offline file package for local extraction or vendor-produced 3D polylines with public distribution rights. Get a quote and written terms permitting retention and publication of that geometry after the subscription ends. The Street Smart API requires a key and login. Its US trial is for evaluation, and neither the trial nor the city's use establishes a grant for Flow Atlas. A contracted file delivery requires no runtime key. No per-site coverage or pricing was confirmed. [NYC Council survey testimony, p. 137](https://legistar.council.nyc.gov/View.ashx?GUID=08ED4219-BE67-4D95-AFC7-A2A77C4425EE&ID=6299333&M=F) · [US provider contact](https://www.cyclomedia.com/en-us) · [Street Smart API](https://developer.cyclomedia.com/our-apis/street-smart/) · [US evaluation license](https://www.cyclomedia.com/sites/default/files/2024-09/Evaluation%20License%20Cyclomedia%20US_Online%20Trial_version%20September%202024.pdf)

**White Plains requires a separate request.** NewYork-Presbyterian Westchester Behavioral Health Center is at **21 Bloomingdale Road, White Plains**. County Buildings **OBJECTID 274916** covers connected wings. Ask the operator for a wing-labeled exterior elevation set or rights-cleared views of the individual wings. Its published grounds plan confirms the complex. The county's Buildings layer supplies plan geometry and height attributes in EPSG:2260. The county's 2025 overhead imagery has half-foot ground pixels, useful for aligning roof outlines. Neither source documents vertical window placement. For other local survey holdings, contact **gisdata@WestchesterCountyNY.gov** with the object ID and request dated oblique or street-level coverage and the applicable reuse terms. Vendor coverage remains unconfirmed here. [NYP campus](https://www.nyp.org/locations/westchester-hudson-valley/westchester-behavioral-health) · [Operator grounds plan](https://www.nyp.org/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2020-09/nyp_westchester_grounds_map.pdf) · [County buildings](https://giswww.westchestergov.com/arcgis/rest/services/DataHub_BasemapPlanimetrics/MapServer/5) · [County orthophoto specifications](https://giswww.westchestergov.com/arcgis/rest/services/MappingWestchesterCounty_AerialPhoto2025/MapServer) · [County GIS contact](https://gis.westchestergov.com/)

**Google Street View and Photorealistic 3D Tiles.** Standard Google Maps Platform terms restrict extracting, rehosting or creating content from Maps content. Map Tiles policies also restrict offline use. A standard Google API key therefore cannot supply the public, locally distributed traces in this brief. [Google Maps Platform terms, §3.2.3](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms) · [Map Tiles policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/policies)

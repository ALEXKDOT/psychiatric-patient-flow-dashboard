# Published psychiatric inpatient capacity by campus

The July 1, 2026 reference covers the 23 campuses in the facility seed. Twenty-one have a supported adult and child/adolescent split. Together they account for 1,879 adult and 230 child/adolescent beds. The remaining two campuses have zero dedicated child/adolescent beds in the shared hospital row. Their individual adult allocations during construction could not be established. The figures describe the psychiatric service's published bed complement. Staffed availability, occupancy, age-specific eligibility, specialty restrictions and admission decisions require separate information. [NYS OMH August 2026 report, Table 5](https://omh.ny.gov/omhweb/transformation/docs/omh-monthly-report-aug-2026.pdf#page=23)

## Source and field definitions

The subtotal of 1,879 adults can be reconstructed as 1,691 across 20 city campuses with known counts, plus 188 at the White Plains psychiatric campus. Staffed vacancies and beds accepting a general ED transfer remain unknown for the region. The code checks the inpatient psychiatric scope, unique campus keys and age totals before using the reference. Each simulated free count belongs to a particular replay time and scenario. [The capacity dataset](../src/campus-capacity.json) is an array keyed by `campus_id`. The three display fields are `adult_beds`, `cap_beds` (child/adolescent psychiatry) and `total_beds`. Null means the campus count could not be resolved. A zero has documentary support. `source_row` identifies the original reporting entity, `method` identifies how its figures were assigned, and `note` records exceptions. The `reporting_entity_*` fields preserve aggregate evidence for audit. The same entity may recur on several campus records. Any campus aggregate should use the three display fields only. A separate `other_dated_site_reference` identifies earlier adult counts at the two under-renovation sites.

DOH provides a separate psychiatric-bed certification table. We selected the seed's physical facility ID, the Bed attribute, the Psychiatric value and the Permanent subtype. We excluded service entries, which use a zero measure to identify a service. `certified_site_total` preserves this independent figure, with the November 18 query date. A site certificate and the OMH age-group report have different scopes and dates. For example, the certificates report 325 at Bellevue, 51 at Harlem and 177 at Elmhurst. OMH reports 316, 44 and 176, respectively. The source notes record both values rather than treating their differences as vacant beds. [DOH certification dataset](https://health.data.ny.gov/Health/Health-Facility-Certification-Information/2g9y-7kqm)

## Verification

The reporting-entity triplets were extracted from PDF text preserving column positions and checked against the rendered table pages. Each complete campus record satisfies adult plus CAP equals its published total. The crosswalk contains exactly the 23 unique seed IDs. Eight campuses have positive child/adolescent capacity in the July snapshot. No patient-level records or operational occupancy data were used.

## Runtime inputs and external access

The exterior acquisition brief identifies the mapped building at all 23 campuses, the exact materials to request and two publicly licensed photographs of the Guggenheim Pavilion. [Façade source brief](FACADE_INPUTS.md)

The checked-in counts, footprint geometry, street data and terrain run locally without a vendor account or API key. Facade windows and parapet lines are illustrative details generated from the walls. A site-accurate pattern of windows would require licensed facade drawings, surveyed models or rights-cleared street-level imagery tied to the building. The demo uses no Google imagery or tiles.

Available means free in the relevant replay pool after occupancy, reservations and turnaround. A boarder is one episode at the sending ED between inpatient disposition and physical departure. Five sites are receive-only in this replay and contribute zero simulated boarders. These scopes apply throughout the dashboard. Neither the historical capacity counts nor the synthetic demand model measures current vacancies, staffing, boarders or admission eligibility.

To show actual operational counts, each participating hospital would need to supply authorized age-specific staffed/open bed inventory, active holds and occupancy, plus inpatient disposition and physical ED departure events or a locally reconciled boarder count. An aggregate feed per facility is sufficient for the panel. Matching site/unit identifiers, feed timestamps, freshness limits and a downtime state would be required before displaying real values. Interface credentials would remain on a server. The public OMH reports and the DOH certification API can update the reference capacity baseline without those feeds.

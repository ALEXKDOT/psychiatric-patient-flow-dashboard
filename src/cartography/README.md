# Local cartography

The regional outlines use the 18 generalized counties from the supplied Census 2025 geography. The detailed tidal mask described below delineates the waterways around the city. See [the geography record](../../docs/research/GEOGRAPHY_README.md).

The fine interior lines are historical Census tract outlines used for geographic texture. They describe neither roads nor service areas. The source is the US Census Bureau's public-domain 2019 1:500,000 CitySDK cartographic boundaries for [New York](https://github.com/uscensusbureau/citysdk/blob/master/v2/GeoJSON/500k/2019/36/tract.json) and [New Jersey](https://github.com/uscensusbureau/citysdk/blob/master/v2/GeoJSON/500k/2019/34/tract.json). Their downloaded SHA256 hashes are f4d44c80df20b6c3fccce84e88695e53aee237a8ec42f7b769c6f3a21d094bb4 and 035c4cacad8736468b69be826bc804dd3a8f5788acab67de34754003bdee4566. This derivative retains exterior rings of polygons whose bounding boxes intersect [-74.45, 40.43, -73.53, 41.16], simplifies them at 0.00013° and rounds coordinates to five decimal places. Both renderers load the same local assets. Regional and water labels are manually positioned for orientation.

MapLibre GL JS is distributed under the BSD 3-Clause License. The fallback uses the browser's native SVG renderer and a Web Mercator projection consistent with the flat MapLibre camera.

Adjacent land outside the handoff's 18 counties uses the [CitySDK national county boundaries, 2019, 1:500,000](https://github.com/uscensusbureau/citysdk/blob/master/v2/GeoJSON/500k/2019/county.json) from the US Census Bureau (public domain), SHA256 819387b23d5a599b60497d6b48099285ccae9d39a3cedf23a7de17f35a192458. New York and neighboring states supply regional context beyond the modeled network. County polygons intersecting [-79.5, 38, -68.8, 44] are dissolved and simplified by 0.0008°, after excluding the handoff's counties and the historical Fairfield County overlapping the modern Western Connecticut region. This layer prevents the edge of a county selection from looking like a shoreline.

The raster print plate uses 2019 public-domain [NY](https://github.com/uscensusbureau/citysdk/blob/master/v2/GeoJSON/500k/2019/36/block-group.json) and [NJ](https://github.com/uscensusbureau/citysdk/blob/master/v2/GeoJSON/500k/2019/34/block-group.json) block-group outlines. The land mask uses the bundled 2025 counties with regional context. Stippling, engraved boundary lines, and shore-parallel printing bands supply surface texture. The shading encodes no measured terrain, population, service catchment, or operational quantities. The original NY and NJ block-group files have SHA256 hashes c47bbead21eef292ce01d8db0ce89e2366aa4fba6d9c5e1e231d9d3f8f8ddaee and 2d45709d470927064767cb2b36ee8382139c908387bff63e537605ccf8a6e76a respectively. To regenerate, run `python src/cartography/make_plate.py path/to/ny-block-group.json path/to/nj-block-group.json` with NumPy, SciPy, and Pillow installed. The app ships the rendered WebP and needs no Python or network requests for cartography.

The generalized county boundaries include administrative water. The tidal mask restores the banks of the Hudson, East and Harlem rivers, docks, islands and bays in both renderers. NYC uses the NYC Department of City Planning's water-clipped borough land polygons, in the [dwillis/nyc-maps source snapshot](https://github.com/dwillis/nyc-maps/blob/598f08a18c0764110846bf069a11249f1bfd09da/boroughs.geojson) (source SHA256 ba883b9d8a194028cb83f2fc75fedbc88cc072bfa863337a554bf972f4d604d5). This historical shoreline is for orientation. The DCP map publisher disclaims accuracy or suitability for a particular use.

The New Jersey and surrounding non-NYC shores use the full-resolution GSHHG shoreline by Paul Wessel and Walter H. F. Smith, distributed in [basemap-data-hires 2.0.0](https://pypi.org/project/basemap-data-hires/2.0.0/) under LGPL-3.0-or-later. The wheel SHA256 is d38c431e9ab348bfda72bc8cfef6b45814c1e5cec69f4aff0754ebb10fedcf43. See [the license](licenses/GSHHG-COPYING.LESSER.txt) and [its incorporated terms](licenses/GSHHG-COPYING.txt). The tidal-water derivative is provided under the same license. The reproducible modification clips the source rings to the regional extent, composites the DCP boroughs within NYC, extracts the water complement, and simplifies it at 0.00006°. To regenerate, supply the unpacked wheel's `mpl_toolkits/basemap_data` directory and the DCP snapshot to `python src/cartography/make_shore.py basemap_data boroughs.geojson` using NumPy and Shapely 2.x, then regenerate the print plate.
